Statement of Purpose

Opu - M.Sc. Software Engineering

Introduction

The day I unboxed my first computer in fifth grade, I wasn't just getting a machine; I was unlocking a portal to endless curiosity. By ninth grade, after exploring digital art, branding, graphic design and learning the fundamentals of web design and development, I landed my first freelance gig. This experience showed me how a creative vision can be transformed into reality, setting my path toward innovation.

Entrepreneurial Ventures

In 2018, I founded BroadBrander while pursuing my studies. What began as a one-person venture eventually served dozens of clients, offering me first-hand experience solving real-world problems with creative digital solutions. This experience taught me valuable practical skills and highlighted the areas where I needed profound technical knowledge.

Academic Foundations

In September 2021, driven by my interest in technology and previous experience, I enrolled in the B.Sc. in Computer Science and Engineering program at the Green University of Bangladesh. My goal was clear: to build a solid theoretical backbone to complement my hands-on experience and prepare myself for the complex challenges that modern software engineering presents.

Professional Experience

As I began my university journey, I had the opportunity to join WeTechPro as a full-stack developer to help cover my educational expenses. Thanks to their student-friendly culture and flexible hours, I was able to thrive in my job while managing my studies. I quickly advanced to the role of Lead Software Engineer, where I managed a team and worked on projects involving web applications and hybrid mobile applications using various technologies such as TypeScript, PHP, Vue, Node, and Capacitor.

One of my most rewarding projects was developing a restaurant management and automation system tailored for small restaurants. I was responsible for designing the system architecture, creating a website, and developing a control panel. I also created software for a handheld POS device that alerts staff and prints order tickets and invoices. This comprehensive system significantly enhanced the operational efficiency of the restaurants.

This experience improved my technical skills and provided valuable insights into leadership, team management, and time management.

Research and Development

I am working on my final year thesis, which focuses on predictive analysis of user interactions using artificial intelligence and machine learning on web platforms. Although my research is still in progress and publication is uncertain, this project has motivated me to enhance my research and analytical skills while deepening my technical knowledge. It acts as a bridge from my professional experience to my aspiration to transform real-world challenges into innovative solutions.

Community Involvement

I have actively volunteered in intra-university programming contests, assisted in organizing the 2022 ICPC Asia Dhaka Regional Contest, and served as the Joint Treasurer of the Green University Computer Club. These experiences have taught me the importance of teamwork, mentorship, and collective growth, significantly contributing to my personal and professional development.

Reflections on My Journey

My undergraduate CGPA of 2.82 reflects the challenges of managing a demanding full-time job alongside my studies. Despite these difficulties, it demonstrates my resilience, strong work ethic, and ability to excel in fast-paced environments. Additionally, my involvement in extracurricular activities has enhanced my leadership and teamwork skills. This journey has deepened my passion for software engineering and emphasized the importance of acquiring advanced knowledge to address complex real-world problems.
